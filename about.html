Who We Are

Building Bright Futures Through Quality Education

NIIST Public School is committed to providing quality education that inspires students to become confident, creative and compassionate individuals. Our school combines academic excellence with strong moral values, preparing students to succeed in an ever-changing world.

We focus on holistic development through innovative teaching methodologies, modern infrastructure, sports, co-curricular activities and technology-enabled learning.

A Message From Our Principal

Education is the foundation upon which the future of every child is built. At NIIST Public School, our mission is to nurture curiosity, encourage creativity, and develop responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

We believe that every child has immense potential. Through dedicated teachers, innovative learning experiences and a supportive environment, we empower students to achieve academic excellence while developing strong character and leadership qualities.
